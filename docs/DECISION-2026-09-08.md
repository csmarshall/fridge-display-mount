## Why this and not the others

- **Design 1 (0.187 in):** $10 more, 2.1 kg heavier, and it has no strut holes so the fallback is
  a recut. Its stiffness advantage (0.022 vs 0.085 mm at the screen edge under a 5 lb press) is
  below anything a finger notices. Nothing else differs. `gauge_magnet_matrix.svg`.
- **Design 2 (struts + clamp):** more parts, floor pads, a clamp under the fridge, and a plate
  that shares nothing with the hook. It is the answer only if the hook fails in use, and design 3
  already carries that answer in four holes. `clamp.html`.
- **Design 4 (stock aluminium):** rejected 2026-09-02, hand drilling.
- **Aluminium plate at SendCutSend:** cheaper on the 2026-08-25 cut-only sweep, but flex is 3x
  steel's at the same gauge, no live quote on the real file, and no reason to reopen the material
  when the steel plate is $187.
- **OSH Cut instead of SendCutSend:** $204.26 coated, $17 more. Their standby tier at $60.93 is
  bent and bare, needs the flat regenerated for their bend table, and you keep the coat. Not
  worth a second vendor flow. `vendors.svg`.
- **Other magnets:** 8 x MM-C-32 is the cheapest set to 6x on a 20 lb grab using the eight
  positions the plate has, on every gauge. Bigger magnets only add cost; smaller ones need four
  holes that are not cut. `magnet_economics.svg`, `gauge_magnet_matrix.svg`.
